# BerryKitchen

> BerryKitchen (Berry Kitchen) was an Indonesian online catering and prepared-meal
> company founded in Jakarta in 2012 by Cynthia Tenggara. It took online orders for
> lunch boxes and bento meals aimed at office workers across Jabodetabek, running the
> full chain itself — supplier sourcing, cooking, packaging, and delivery. It was
> backed by East Ventures and 500 Startups (now 500 Global), and was acquired by
> Yummy Corp in 2019, with the team folded into Yummy Corp's Yummybox unit and the
> founder subsequently exiting. The company operated no public developer platform.

## Status

- Acquired by Yummy Corp (2019); operated thereafter as part of Yummybox.
- The berrykitchen.com domain is no longer operated by the company: the apex
  redirects to an unrelated storefront and the www host returns a Cloudflare
  error 1000. There is no live company website, developer portal, or documentation.

## APIs

None. BerryKitchen published no public API, no OpenAPI/AsyncAPI description, no SDKs,
no MCP server, and no developer documentation. This profile is catalogued for
historical and portfolio-graph purposes only.
